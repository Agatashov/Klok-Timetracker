Support

Help keep Klok free.

Klok has no ads, no accounts, and no subscription. It's free to download and free to use. If it's saved you time, here's exactly what an optional donation funds, and a couple of ways to help that don't cost anything.

Where it goes

  1. 01 Apple Developer Program The $99/year fee that keeps Klok notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper doesn't flag it on a fresh Mac.
  2. 02 Development time Every item on the roadmap competes with a day job. Support buys back hours to actually ship it.
  3. 03 Staying independent No telemetry and no "free with limits" tier down the line. Support is what makes a one-time build sustainable instead of a subscription.
