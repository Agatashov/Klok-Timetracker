Klok is a native Mac app for freelancers and consultants who want simple, local time tracking — with statements you can share and get paid from, using a Google Sheet you already have.

01 — Track

Track

Start a timer, or log time manually, against a client and project. Everything stays on your Mac — nothing is uploaded anywhere while you work.

02 — Statement

Generate a statement

When you're ready to bill, pick a client, a project (or all of them), and a date range. Klok builds a clean statement from your tracked time.

03 — Get paid

Share & get paid

Klok pushes that statement straight into a Google Sheet you've linked. Send your client the link — that's the bill.
