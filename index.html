Setup

Connect
Google
Sheets.

Klok keeps your data on your Mac. When you bill a client, it puts the statement in a Google Sheet you link once.

  1. 1 Create a Google Sheet New or existing — at sheets.google.com.
  2. 2 Copy its link Copy the sheet's URL from your browser's address bar.
  3. 3 Paste it into Klok Open a client's settings and paste the link into Statement Sheet.
  4. 4 Add a time log sheet Optional. Repeat with a second sheet as a separate Time Log Sheet for raw tracked hours.
  5. 5 Approve once The first time you generate a statement, Klok asks permission to write to the sheet. You won't be asked again.

Done. Every statement lands as a new tab in that sheet, ready to share.
